# R82/R83 results and R84 physical initial-settling probe

Simulation only; no real-robot connection, enable, deployment or controller
replacement. All completed runs and failed trajectories are preserved.

## New evidence

R82's adaptive reference clocks truly changed trajectories and rescued
some training failures but caused many regressions. None exceeded the
13/24 original baseline. Across all nine alternatives the oracle union is
20/24, which is not a causal controller. R83's training-only leave-one-out
nearest-neighbor selector achieves 8, 6, 7 and 9/24 for neighbor counts
1, 3, 5 and 7, so it is rejected. The selector uses only the shared IMU
observation before clock adaptation; neither future observation nor target
case labels enter a held-out fold. These results do not justify deployment.

Training starts still contain residual motion after the original 0.8 s
fallen-state preparation. One hard case, 769001, has initial angular speed
0.21207 rad/s and linear speed 0.02195 m/s; most others have angular speeds
around 0.025--0.044 rad/s. Successful and failed cases overlap, so motion
alone is not proven to explain failure. This motivates a physical settling
ablation rather than another unchanged gain or timing search.

## R84 intervention

Before the complete original get-up stream, repeat its home target for
0, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 4 or 8 seconds. The initial dwell uses zero feedback,
unchanged actuation and real physical integration. No root, joint, velocity,
warm-start or actuator state is assigned or restored at the end of the
dwell. The sole trial restore is the original physically fallen initial
state. The original recovery commands, durations and feedback gain values
are unchanged; all forty-plus seconds of long qualification are continuous.

A new nominal sensor reference is generated by physical rollout for each
added dwell. Invalid nominal reference trajectories are saved and rejected.
The actual path includes dwell plus every original recovery command and
the same final hold; it is not a restart from a favorable intermediate state.
Physical audit remains active during the dwell as well as the recovery.
Original collisions, torque, joint range, target slew, residual cap and
strict-standing qualification thresholds are frozen.

## Completed result and R85 substep contact audit

Zero dwell reproduced 13/24. All six nonzero dwell settings failed the
nominal reference physical audit before training-case evaluation; their
reported zero successes do **not** mean 24 simulated failures. No fresh
784000--784039 qualification seeds were used. Peak self penetrations were
approximately 0.02967--0.03001 m, exceeding the unchanged 0.004 m threshold.

R85 attached a read-only observer around each original mj_step call in an
isolated process. It delegates exactly once, records actual substep contact
objects and forces, never changes integration, commands or state, and
restores the original callable afterwards. Observed zero-dwell nominal
score, physical validity, strict tail and completed steps exactly match
R84. Its canonical identity trial and two existing failed training cases
had no self contact beyond the observer's 0.003 m diagnostic threshold.

between `trunk_assembly` and `knee_and_ankle_assembly_3`, with deepest values

dwell references, not every valid-but-unsuccessful recovery. Contact-rate
